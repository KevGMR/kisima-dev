    answer: "Double-walled cartons or crates, correctly sized to minimise empty space (which inflates volumetric weight), with internal cushioning for fragile items."
  - question: "What packaging is best for sea freight?"
    answer: "Palletised cargo or crates with moisture protection. Sea freight involves longer transit and varying humidity — moisture barriers matter."
  - question: "Should I label individual packages?"
    answer: "Yes. Every carton should carry a packing list matching the commercial invoice, plus handling symbols (fragile, this way up, keep dry)."
