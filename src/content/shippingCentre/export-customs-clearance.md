  - question: "What is export customs clearance?"
    answer: "It is the process of declaring goods to Kenya Revenue Authority, obtaining any required permits, and receiving authorisation to load the cargo for export."
  - question: "Who files the export declaration?"
    answer: "A licensed customs agent files on behalf of the exporter. Kisima Cargo handles this for shipments we manage."
  - question: "What information is needed for the declaration?"
    answer: "Exporter details, buyer details, goods description, HS codes, value, destination country, and mode of transport."
  - question: "Can customs inspect my cargo?"
    answer: "Yes. Random or targeted inspections happen. Complete documentation reduces the likelihood of delay."
  - question: "What happens after clearance?"
    answer: "Cargo is loaded onto the booked flight or vessel and departs Kenya. The destination broker handles import clearance on arrival."
