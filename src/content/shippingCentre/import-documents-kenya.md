sections:
  - question: "What documents are needed to import into Kenya?"
    answer: "At minimum: commercial invoice, packing list, and transport document (bill of lading or airway bill). Additional documents depend on the commodity."
  - question: "What is a commercial invoice?"
    answer: "A document from your supplier that lists the goods, quantities, unit prices, total value, and terms of sale. Customs uses it to assess duty and VAT."
  - question: "What is a packing list?"
    answer: "A document listing what is in each package — item, quantity, weight, dimensions. It helps customs physically match cargo to the invoice."
  - question: "What is a Certificate of Origin?"
    answer: "A document certifying the country where goods were produced. It can affect duty rates under trade agreements."
  - question: "What is CoC / PVoC?"
    answer: "Certificate of Conformity / Pre-Export Verification of Conformity — required for certain regulated products under KEBS rules (electronics, some toys, cosmetics, etc.)."
    explanation: "If your goods fall under these rules, you obtain the certificate in the origin country before shipping. We advise you per shipment and help coordinate with suppliers."
  - question: "What is an IDF?"
    answer: "Import Declaration Form — filed with Kenya customs to declare the import before cargo arrives. Kisima Cargo files this on your behalf."
  - question: "Can I ship without complete documents?"
    answer: "No. Missing or incorrect documents cause delays and possible penalties. We review documentation before cargo is shipped so issues are caught early."
