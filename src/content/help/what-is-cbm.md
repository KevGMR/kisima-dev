  - question: "What is CBM?"
    answer: "CBM stands for cubic metre — a measure of volume equal to 1m × 1m × 1m. Sea freight is charged per CBM."
  - question: "How is CBM calculated?"
    answer: "Multiply length × width × height in metres, then multiply by quantity. The result is your total CBM."
    bullets:

  - question: "Example: two 1.2×0.8×1m cartons"

  - question: "What is LCL?"
    answer: "LCL stands for Less than Container Load — your cargo shares a container with other shippers' cargo. Charged per CBM."
  - question: "What is FCL?"
    answer: "FCL stands for Full Container Load — you book an entire container. Charged per container, not per CBM. FCL usually makes sense above a certain volume threshold."
  - question: "Does CBM include packaging?"
    answer: "Yes. CBM is measured on the outer dimensions of your packed cargo, including pallets if used."
